Final Percentage Calculation

The final percentage is calculated using:

Closed Book Assessment: 80%
Open Book Assessment: 10%
Other assessments: 10%

Missing assessment values are treated as zero.

Grade Boundaries

80% and above = A
65% and above = B
50% and above = C
Below 50% = C

Class Ranking

Your class rank is based on your final weighted percentage compared with the other students in the class.

Rankings may change as additional assessment results are added. This page will be updated throughout the year.
